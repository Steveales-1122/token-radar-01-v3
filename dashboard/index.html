How the price is built

implied value = TVL × category multiple × momentum × maturity × risk. The multiple is the median market-cap / TVL ratio of the protocols in this snapshot that already have a token, in the same category. A category with fewer than 3 priced peers uses the global median instead, and the table says so. The low/high range comes from the same peers' 25th and 75th percentiles. Momentum is the 7-day TVL change, maturity is the age since listing, risk is the chain spread.

This is a model, not a promise: a high implied value means the market pays that much for comparable TVL elsewhere — it does not mean a token will launch, or at that price.

Below the floor, the board only carries protocols that could plausibly launch a token of their own. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of a token that already trades, versioned copies and centralised exchanges are left out — WBTC is Bitcoin on Ethereum, not a protocol waiting on a launch — using the same rule the snapshot's own watchlist uses.
